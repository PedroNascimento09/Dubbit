# Acceptable use

Dubbit is a tool for translating and subtitling media that you have the right to use. This page is a statement of intent, not a restriction added to the licence. The Apache-2.0 licence governs the code, and an open source project cannot fully enforce how the software is used.

## Not allowed

- Fraud, scams or impersonation of a real person or organisation.
- Non-consensual voice cloning, or making someone appear to say something they did not say.
- Harassment, threats or targeted abuse.
- Disinformation, including content intended to interfere with elections or public safety.
- Processing media you do not have permission to use, or redistributing it in breach of copyright.
- Circumventing platform terms, paywalls or technical protection measures.

## Your responsibility

You are responsible for the media, voices and translations you create, and for the rights attached to them. Check the law where you live before publishing translated or synthetic audio.

## Project safeguards

- Voice cloning and lip sync are not part of the current build.
- Any future voice feature will be off by default, require an explicit consent confirmation and keep synthetic-audio metadata on by default.
- Subtitles and project files are produced locally, with no upload.

## Limits

Because the source code is open, these rules cannot be enforced technically. They describe what the project stands for, and the maintainers will not support or promote uses that break them.
