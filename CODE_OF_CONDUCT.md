# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want Dubbit to be a place where anyone can contribute without being harassed or dismissed. We welcome people of every background, experience level and identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and kind. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith and ask before assigning motives.
- Give useful reviews: explain the problem, the reason and a possible fix.
- Respect that maintainers have limited time and make the final call on scope.
- Keep discussions on the topic of the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory comments about identity, background or ability.
- Sexual attention or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Using project spaces to promote fraud, impersonation or non-consensual voice use.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, discussions, issues, pull requests, review comments and any space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through the contact options on the maintainer's repository profile. Do not open a public issue about a conduct problem.

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer will investigate, hear the people involved and choose a response proportional to the behaviour, from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for enforcing this document and may remove content or restrict participation when behaviour harms the community. Decisions are explained to the people involved.
